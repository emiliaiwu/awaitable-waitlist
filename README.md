# 🚀 Awaitable – Waitlist Tracker for Everything You’re Waiting On

**Awaitable** helps you track, manage, and stay updated on all the product waitlists you've joined — in one simple, organized dashboard.

No more digging through emails. No more guessing if that app ever launched. Just clarity.

---

## 🧠 Why Awaitable?

People join dozens of waitlists — AI tools, early startups, beta apps — and quickly lose track. Awaitable solves this by:

- Saving every waitlist you join
- Showing live status for each app (in development, beta, launched, etc.)
- Notifying you when apps send invites or updates
- Letting you tag, sort, and note why you joined each one

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Smart Waitlist Inbox** – Automatically detects and organizes waitlist-related emails  
- 📋 **App Status Tracking** – See if an app is in development, beta, launched, paused, or cancelled  
- 🔔 **Invite Alerts** – Get notified when you're invited or when a product goes live  
- 🏷️ **Tags & Notes** – Add context to each app: category, use case, reminders  
- 📊 **Dashboard Insights** – View stats like oldest wait, most recent activity, total joined  
- 🔐 **Private by Default** – Your data is yours. Nothing shared unless you export it.
